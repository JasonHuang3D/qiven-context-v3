- **Parameters:** none
- **Refuses:** two cases of one name, or a name that begins or ends with its dot, and a case or an argument it does not know: usage, status 2; a case that leaves an allocation of its own held: the case fails. The listing (`list_tests.py`): a left-out name that matches no test, `--leave-out <name>: no test of that id or package`, which fails the build. The watcher: no `--limit` or `--name`, or no command after `--`, its usage, status 2; a case that cannot start, `watch.py: <test>: the case cannot start: <reason>`, clipped, status 2
- **The watcher:** `python -I -X faulthandler foundation/devkit/test/runner/watch.py --limit <s> --name <test> -- <command>...` runs the case and names how it ended. It sets `QIVEN_WATCHER`, its own process id, and, with `QIVEN_BUILD` naming the build, `QIVEN_DUMP_DIR`, the build's `dumps/`, and `QIVEN_LOG`, `logs/<test>.jsonl`, each directory made. It sets the quiet load-error mode first (`system.quiet_load_errors`), which the case inherits; the case inherits its standard output, its standard input closed, and its standard error is relayed line by line. Then one line on standard error names the status by `status`'s table, and on a library missing at load the imports found nowhere the loader looks, by `imports`. It exits with the case's status, a POSIX signal as 128 and its number, and after every case bounds the build's `dumps/` by `dumps.py`. Past `--limit`, `debugger.hang_stacks` (the pinned cdb, or a CI image's, unpinned): `hang: past its limit of <s> s, <n> threads` and every thread's stack, at most 16 threads and 200 lines, and `<binary>-<pid>-hang.dmp` in `dumps/`; with no debugger, `hang: no debugger, no stacks` and the reason. Then it ends the case's tree, names what it wrote and the case, `status: 124 hang: ...; <written>; <test> past <s> s`, and exits 124. Its worst case, against the 30 s CTest leaves it after a case's limit (`ctest_tests.cmake`'s `AFTER_LIMIT`), is in `debugger`'s charter (`foundation/devkit/python/README.md`): `HANG_SECONDS`, 12 s, reckoned beside `READER_WAIT` in `watch.py` and given to `hang_stacks`, finding the debugger and its stacks together, `process`'s ending of cdb, the case's ending and `READER_WAIT`, 5 s, for the case's stream. Its consumers: `ctest_tests.cmake`, every test's command; `binaries.py`'s `WATCHER`, which `run_watched` runs; `tools/debug.py`, which drops the watcher from a test's command by its name
- **The dumps' bound:** `bound(directory, pid=None, kept=DUMPS_KEPT)`, `DUMPS_KEPT` 8: a dump is a file `*.dmp` of the directory, nothing else read or removed; ordered by its last write and then its name, the newest `kept` stay, and every dump of process `pid` (`<binary>-<pid>.dmp`, `<binary>-<pid>-hang.dmp`), so that the dump a case's report or a hang's lines name is never removed. `hang_dump(pid, binary, directory)` is the one place the hang dump's name is formed, `None` where no directory is given. It answers a line for each dump removed, `dumps: removed <name>, past the newest <kept>`, or that cannot be removed, `dumps: cannot remove <name>: <reason>`; one another process removed first gives no line, and no directory none. `run_watched` drops those lines from the watcher's standard error, another case's dump, written beside, giving them in any run
- **`run_watched(command, name, env=None, cwd=None, limit=TIME_BOUND)`:** the command behind the watcher, by this interpreter under `-I -X faulthandler`, as CTest runs it, `--limit` the limit given, `TIME_BOUND`, 10 s, unless given; it polls every `POLL`, 0.1 s, for the visible windows of the watcher and its children, ends the watcher's tree where it still runs past the limit and twice `AFTER`, 30 s, and answers `(status, out, err, seconds, seen)`, the output as bytes. Its consumers: diag's `tests/test_faults.py`; `build`'s `tests/test_hardened.py`; `tools/tests/test_debug.py`; this module's `tests/test_hang.py`, which gives its own limits
- **A Python test's skip:** `unittest` ends a run of skipped tests alone with 0 and prints `OK (skipped=<n>)`; `ctest_tests.cmake` gives each Python test `SKIP_REGULAR_EXPRESSION` `OK \(skipped=[0-9]+\)[^a-z]+status: 0 passed`, the sentence and then the watcher's status line, and the listed command's `-v` names each skip's reason. A failure prints `FAILED`, and a run of passes and skips together `OK (skipped=<n>)` as well, a test's id selecting one test alone. Its limit: a passing test that prints that sentence itself is read as skipped, and a run whose watcher prints a line with a lower-case letter between the sentence and the status line (the dumps' bound's, past `DUMPS_KEPT`) as passed
- **Compatibility, extensibility and performance:** Windows built; POSIX and macOS on paper through `process`, `system`, `debugger` and the 128 and a signal's number of the watcher's exit, its imports' reader PE's alone, no Linux build; Linux's symbolizing and loader path retired (`tools/README.md`'s Retired section). A new kind of test is one more call of `qiven_add_test` in `ctest_tests.cmake`, a new check one more macro over `check`. The watcher loads `answer`, `debugger`, `imports`, `process`, `status`, `system` and the dumps' bound at its start, in front of every test: measured once by a probe in scratch (interpreter 3.15.0 under `-I -B`, the median of 15 runs, while `debugger` still imported `build_tree`, before `1612fde`), a case behind it took 115.6 ms where the interpreter alone took 25.6 ms, about 90 ms a test; our imports 27.6 ms of it, `debugger` 20.9 ms, `imports` 4.6 ms, `answer` 1.2 ms, the dumps' bound 0.9 ms
- **A fake and errors:** none: the watcher stands in front of the real case. `tests/test_hang.py` runs it with a stand-in cdb (`cdb.cmd`) on `PATH` for a CI image's path and a sleeping interpreter as the case (`Hang`, label `fake`), and K8, a real hang under the pinned cdb (`HangStacks`, label `debugger`); every test CTest runs stands behind it, and `test_wiring.py`'s `test_properties` holds each test's command to `WATCHER`; the dumps' bound has no test of its own, every watched run reaching it. The watcher's own lines name it: `watch.py: <test>: the case cannot start: <reason>`; `hang: no debugger, no stacks` and `hang: <reason>`; the status line's `; its imports cannot be read` or `; not found: <library>, ...`, each line that carries a name, a path or an error's words held within `answer`'s `LINE_POINTS` by `answer.clip`, the cut named, its stacks bounded by `debugger`
